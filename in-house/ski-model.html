How it works

  • Ruling places out A place is ruled out, with the reason shown, when it is too far, has too little terrain above your minimum elevation, has a pass closure likely, or (in backcountry mode) has High or Extreme avalanche danger or no avalanche forecast. Resort and nordic modes show avalanche danger as a note instead.
  • Then a weighted score. Fresh snow, snow quality (snow-to-liquid ratio), wind, visibility and cloud, vertical, drive time and road reliability are each scored 0 to 1 and averaged with the weights you set.
  • When to go. Each card also picks the best four-hour stretch of the day (8 am to 4 pm) from the hourly forecast. Rain counts most against a window, active snow less for skiers (more for hiking and biking), gusts above about 15 mph add to it, and so does temperature (outside the comfortable range for hiking and biking; for snow activities warmth above about 30 F counts against a window much more than cold, so cooling off beats warming up); for skiers, snow that has already fallen breaks ties. The window has no rating of its own; the score only picks it. “Improving”, “stable” or “deteriorating” compares the three hours after the window with the three hours before it, so a storm moving out reads as improving and one moving in as deteriorating, even if it arrives just after you are done. Where the forecast has no hours on one side (a window at the edge of the day), the window’s own first or second half stands in for that side. Timing is hourly for about the first two days; after that the forecast only gives rain in 6-hour blocks, so the window is approximate and is marked as such.
  • Where the data come from (beta): the National Blend of Models for snow (24-hour median and range), snow-to-liquid ratio, snow level, wind, cloud and visibility at one point near each area, for the next four days. Pass delay chances come from seven winters of I-90 delays (WSDOT Snoqualmie Winter Operations Study) applied to the other highway passes, and the Paradise road follows the National Park Service’s winter opening rules. A bot also logs pass closures and the Paradise gate each morning so we can improve this. Not connected yet: NWAC avalanche forecasts (they start in late November, so backcountry mode is blocked until then) and live traffic. Drive times are estimates.
