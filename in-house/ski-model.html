{{ site.ski_model_name }}: Ski Recommendation Model

Our forecast for the weekend, boiled down to a recommendation: where to go, and when, for the kind of day you want. The name is a working title.

What it will do

  • Score each area and day from the forecast: new snow, snow level, temperature, wind, cloud cover and visibility.
  • Rank the areas for the weekend, and say why.
  • Check itself: we will score its past picks against what was observed, the same way the forecast evaluation scores our snowfall ranges.

Still to decide

What counts as a good day for different kinds of skiers (powder, corn, a touring day, a resort day), how to weigh each factor, and which data it uses.
