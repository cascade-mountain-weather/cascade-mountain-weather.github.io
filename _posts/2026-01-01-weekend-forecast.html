🗣️ Word of the Week: Vorticity

What it means: Vorticity measures the rotation of the atmosphere. Positive vorticity advection (PVA) is when spin is transported into a region, forcing air to rise.

Why you care: Positve vorticity advection forces air upwards which helps wring moisture out of the atmosphere, increasing clouds and precipitation. This is a reason why troughs—features high in PVA—often coincide with heavier mountain snowfall.
