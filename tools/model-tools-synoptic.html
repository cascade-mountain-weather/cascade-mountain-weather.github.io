Upper Air and Surface Weather

The overall large-scale weather pattern at both upper-levels and the surface. We include both deterministic, AI, and ensemble model output at several spatial scales from the GFS, ECMWF, UW WRF, CW3E, and WeatherNext3 models (plus synoptic panels from the University of Utah weather portal). Forecast duration spans 5- to 15-days, depending on the model. Pick a product and a model, then drag the slider or press play. Times are shown in Pacific time with the model’s Z (UTC) time beside them.
