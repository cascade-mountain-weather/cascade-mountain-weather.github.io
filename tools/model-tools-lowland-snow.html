Lowland Snow Tools

Will it snow in the lowlands? Forecasts and observations from the University of Washington’s SnowWatch for Puget Sound and western Washington. These products include the real-time ensemble (6-hr snowfall), the UW-WRF Puget Sound region snow forecast, the snow level trend, the freezing level over Seattle, and near-surface temperatures. Drag the slider or press play where there is a time series. Times are shown in Pacific time with the Z (UTC) time beside them.
