Precipitation Tools

How much, and when? Choose an area and a product. Ensemble plots show many model runs at once, so the spread between lines is your confidence: tight means the models agree, wide means they do not. The Northwest region view has maps of snow, precipitation and clouds from the HRRR, RRFS, UW WRF and the Utah ensemble. For rain versus snow, see the Freezing & Snow Level tools.
