Seasonal Outlooks

The odds for the next few months as a whole. A seasonal outlook cannot say whether a given storm will deliver, but it can tilt expectations for the winter: a little warmer or colder, a little wetter or drier.

ECMWF seasonal (SEAS5)

The ECMWF seasonal system gives the odds for the next few three-month seasons. Pick a season to compare temperature, precipitation and the 500 hPa pattern, or pick a product to compare the seasons.
