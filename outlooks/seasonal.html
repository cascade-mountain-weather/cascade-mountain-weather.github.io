Seasonal Outlooks

The odds for the next few months as a whole. A seasonal outlook cannot say whether a given storm will deliver, but it can tilt expectations for the winter: a little warmer or colder, a little wetter or drier.

Climate Prediction Center (NOAA)

The CPC three-month outlook for the first lead period (the next three-month season), for temperature and precipitation.

Source: NOAA Climate Prediction Center. The written discussion explains the reasoning behind the maps.
