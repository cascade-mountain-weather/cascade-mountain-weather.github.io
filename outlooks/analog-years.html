Our historical analysis: we line up past winters by the climate patterns that were in place, and look at how the Cascades snowpack and storms actually turned out. The working title is a placeholder.

The idea

Each season starts with a state of the ocean and atmosphere: the teleconnections. If we find past seasons that began with a similar state, how did their winters compare to normal for snowpack, temperature and storm activity? That is the analog method.

What we plan to build

Data

SNOTEL snow water equivalent, snow depth and temperature at the stations we already track for each area, matched with monthly values of ENSO, PDO, PNA and other indices.

Analysis

How the snowpack grew in past winters that look like this one, how wide the range of outcomes was, and how often the pattern broke.

Honesty about the limits

With only a few decades of good records, analog years give a range of possibilities, not a forecast. We will show how much each pattern actually explains.
