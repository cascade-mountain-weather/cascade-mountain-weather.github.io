Sub-seasonal Outlooks

Roughly week 2 through week 6. Skill is low, so read these as shifts in the odds toward warmer or colder, wetter or drier than normal, not as a day-by-day forecast.

Climate Prediction Center (NOAA)

The CPC 6–10 and 8–14 day outlooks show the probability of above or below normal temperature and precipitation. Colors show the favored category and how strongly, not how much.

Source: NOAA Climate Prediction Center. Also see the week 3–4 outlook.

What we plan to add
